LEGAL · TERMS

Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to customized project reports, business plans and related financial documentation ordered through StartProject.

1. Customized service

Every report is prepared against the information, requirements and project details submitted by the customer. StartProject does not sell ready-made or copy-paste project reports.

2. Information provided by the customer

The customer is responsible for providing complete, accurate and current information, including project cost, business details, location, financial assumptions and intended use of the report. We prepare the report based on the information received.

3. Delivery timeline

The stated 2–10 working hour delivery window begins after payment confirmation and after the required information has been received. If information is incomplete or additional clarification is required, the delivery timeline may be extended until the required inputs are available.

4. Review and revisions

After delivery, the customer may reply to the report email with reasonable changes or corrections relating to the information originally provided. Revised PDFs may be issued where applicable. Changes that materially alter the scope of the original assignment may require additional work or charges.

5. No approval guarantee

A project report is a professional planning and documentation service. It does not guarantee approval of a bank loan, subsidy, PMEGP application, grant, government permission, investor funding, land allocation or any other third-party decision. Final decisions are made by the relevant bank, authority, institution or investor.

6. Scope and responsibility

StartProject is responsible for preparing the agreed documentation based on the submitted information. The customer remains responsible for using the report appropriately and for independently verifying information where a bank, authority, consultant or professional advisor requires it.

LEGAL · REFUNDS

Refund Policy

Because StartProject reports are customized and work is undertaken specifically for each customer, our refund policy is based on the stage of the order.

1. Before work begins

If an order has been placed but work has not yet commenced, please contact us immediately. Any refund request at this stage will be reviewed based on whether work, processing or third-party costs have already been incurred.

2. After work has commenced

Once the order has been confirmed, the required information has been received and work has commenced, payments are generally non-refundable because the service involves customized research, financial preparation, report drafting and project-specific work.

3. Delivery and revisions

A request for reasonable corrections or revisions to information supplied by the customer does not by itself qualify as a refund. Where applicable, the customer may reply to the delivery email with the required changes and we can issue a revised PDF.

4. Incorrect or incomplete information

If the customer provides incorrect, incomplete or materially changed information after work has started, the resulting delay, revision or additional work does not automatically create a refund entitlement. Additional scope may require a separate commercial discussion.

5. Delivery issues

If there is a genuine issue with delivery of the agreed report, please contact us through the registered email or contact details used for the order so that we can review and resolve the issue.
